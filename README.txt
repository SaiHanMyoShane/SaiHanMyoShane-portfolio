## Featured Work

### WISE-StudentLLM-Bench

WISE-StudentLLM-Bench is a preliminary technical work sample focused on evaluating student-facing large language model responses.

The project explores areas including:

* hallucination and misinformation
* privacy and academic integrity
* overreliance on AI
* unsafe or misleading advice
* reasoning quality
* output-format reliability
* student-friendly AI safety guidance

The project is presented as an early technical and research-oriented work sample rather than a validated benchmark.

**GitHub repository:**
https://github.com/SaiHanMyoShane/WISE-StudentLLM-Bench
