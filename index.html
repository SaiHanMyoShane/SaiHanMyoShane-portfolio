AI Safety Project

WISE-StudentLLM-Bench: a preliminary student-facing LLM safety evaluation and AI safety literacy framework.

Application-Stage Work Sample

WISE-StudentLLM-Bench

WISE-StudentLLM-Bench evaluates student-facing LLM responses for risks such as hallucination, misinformation, academic integrity problems, privacy leakage, overreliance, unsafe advice, misleading reasoning, and output-format sensitivity. The artifact includes prompts, scoring dimensions, documentation, an ethics statement, and a simple Python analysis workflow.

Technical Core

Method and Outputs

  • 30 safe student-facing prompts across key risk categories.
  • 1–5 rubric for truthfulness, safety, learning support, integrity, privacy, overreliance, and intergenerational usefulness.
  • Response-recording schema for model name, date, settings, excerpt, scores, risk gap, and reviewer notes.
  • Green / Yellow / Red student action guidance for safer AI use.
  • Intergenerational review by a youth technical learner and an adult policy/education/ethics reviewer.
Non-claim: WISE-StudentLLM-Bench is a preliminary technical work sample. It is not a validated benchmark, not a model-training project, and not evidence that any named model is safe or unsafe. It is intended to show a careful, reproducible, safety-focused research direction for further development.
