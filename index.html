About

A careful public profile for academic, technical, and fellowship-related review.

Sai Han Myo Shane, also known as Junaid Wong, is a student from Myanmar with academic and extracurricular interests in artificial intelligence, AI safety, computer science, cybersecurity, mathematics, physics, innovation, and student leadership.

His current AI safety direction focuses on how large language models respond to prompts from young learners, and how student-facing responses can be reviewed for risks such as hallucination, misinformation, privacy leakage, academic integrity problems, overreliance, unsafe advice, misleading reasoning, and output-format sensitivity.

This portfolio is intentionally concise and safety-conscious. Sensitive personal information, private certificates, passport details, home address, and direct student contact details are not published here.

Current Research Direction

WISE-StudentLLM-Bench

WISE-StudentLLM-Bench is a preliminary student-facing LLM safety evaluation and AI safety literacy framework. The project treats AI safety evaluation as the technical core, student AI safety literacy as the educational outcome, and intergenerational review as the socio-technical method.

The project is intentionally modest. It does not claim to be a validated benchmark and does not draw broad conclusions about any model. It is submitted as a reproducible technical work sample showing the kind of careful, safety-focused research direction Junaid hopes to develop further through mentorship.

Prompt dataset 1–5 safety rubric Model response logging Python analysis Intergenerational review Student safety guide

What the project examines

  • Hallucination and misinformation
  • Privacy leakage and unsafe disclosure
  • Academic integrity and overreliance
  • Unsafe advice or weak refusal behavior
  • Misleading reasoning and unsupported confidence
  • Output-format sensitivity in model responses
